iOS and iPadOS

Developer Bo Bo · Effective November 25, 2026

Photos and permissions

The Clean and Swipe screens ask for access to your Apple Photos library to find similar or blurry photos, screenshots, and large videos, and to let you review and remove items you select. SnapClean works within the access you grant. You can change access in iOS or iPadOS Settings. The Toolbox uses Apple’s photo picker to open items you choose. The system may ask for permission when you save results back to Photos.

Scanning, photo comparison, compression, editing, PDF creation, and text recognition run on your device. SnapClean does not send your photos, videos, metadata, or recognized text to the developer. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple Photos may retrieve cloud-only items and sync library changes according to your settings. When you share, export, or copy a result, the selected destination or system clipboard handles that content.

Storage and removal

SnapClean stores cached photo signatures, review status, cleanup totals, and settings in its app storage. Selected inputs and generated outputs may be held temporarily in the app’s temporary storage. You can clear the scan index in SnapClean Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app storage. Saved copies, shared files, and any system backups are managed separately. Confirmed deletions go through Apple Photos, which manages recovery in Recently Deleted.

App data collection and contact

The iOS and iPadOS app does not collect data through accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or a developer-operated media server. If you email support at longdabo@gmail.com, we use the details you choose to send to answer your request. You can also use this address for privacy questions and requests. Please keep private photos, videos, and recognized text out of your message.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no analytics scripts or cookies. GitHub can log visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We publish policy changes here and update the effective date.

照片和权限

「清理」和「滑动整理」页面会请求访问 Apple「照片」，用于查找相似照片、模糊照片、截图和大视频，让你检查并移除选中的项目。SnapClean 仅在你授权的范围内访问相册。你可以在 iOS 或 iPadOS 设置中更改授权。「工具箱」通过 Apple 的照片选择器读取你选中的项目。保存结果时，系统可能请求写入照片图库的权限。

扫描、照片比对、压缩、编辑、创建 PDF 和文字识别均在设备上运行。SnapClean 不会向开发者发送你的照片、视频、元数据或识别文字。启用 iCloud 照片后，Apple「照片」可能按照系统设置获取仅存于 iCloud 的项目并同步相册变更。主动分享、导出或复制时，内容由你选择的目的地或系统剪贴板处理。

存储与删除

SnapClean 在应用存储中保存照片特征缓存、查看状态、清理统计和设置。选中的素材和生成的文件可能暂存在应用的临时存储中。你可以在 SnapClean 设置中清除扫描索引。删除应用会移除其本机存储。另存的副本、已分享的文件及系统备份需分别管理。确认删除后，由 Apple「照片」处理，并管理「最近删除」中的恢复选项。

Android

X App Team · Effective and last updated November 24, 2026

Photos, videos, and permissions

SnapClean uses Android’s photo and video permissions to scan the media library you allow it to access. It reads files and metadata such as names, folders, sizes, and dates to find similar or blurry photos, screenshots, and large videos, and to help you review and clean up your library. On supported Android versions you can grant access to selected media. Older Android versions use storage permissions for reading and, where required, saving or deleting media. You can change access in Android settings.

Media scanning, photo fingerprints and sharpness scores, compression, format conversion, cropping, watermarking, EXIF removal, PDF creation, and optical character recognition (OCR) run on your device. X App Team does not receive your media, photo metadata, or recognized text. When you save a file, send an export to another app, or copy recognized text, the chosen location, receiving app, or Android clipboard handles that content. A file provider you choose may have its own privacy practices.

Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit recognizes text on your device. Google states that the SDK sends diagnostics and usage metrics to Google over HTTPS. These can include device and app details, a per-installation identifier, feature events and error codes, and performance metrics such as processing time or input size. The image and recognized text stay on your device during recognition. See ML Kit Terms and Privacy and ML Kit’s Android data disclosure.

SnapClean has no X App Team account, developer-operated media server, or advertising SDK. X App Team does not sell your personal information.

Local storage, backups, and deletion

SnapClean keeps its scan index, photo fingerprints, review status, preferences, and cleanup totals in Android’s app storage. Temporary files may be kept in the app cache. The scan database is excluded from Android cloud backup. Other eligible app data, such as settings, may be backed up or restored according to your Android backup and device migration settings.

You can clear the scan index in SnapClean settings. Android’s app storage settings let you clear the app cache or app data. Uninstalling removes the app’s local storage. Files saved to your library, copies you shared, and system backups are managed separately. On Android 11 and later, SnapClean requests system confirmation to move media to the trash, where Android controls retention and recovery. On older versions, deletion may be permanent.

SnapClean 隐私政策

开发者 X App Team · Android · com.xapp.waveleap.photoclean
生效及最近更新日期 2026 年 11 月 24 日

照片、视频和权限

SnapClean 使用 Android 的照片和视频权限扫描你授权访问的媒体库。应用会读取文件以及名称、文件夹、大小和日期等元数据，用于识别相似照片、模糊照片、截图和大视频，并协助你查看和清理相册。在支持的 Android 版本上，你可以只授权访问选定的媒体。较早版本通过存储权限读取媒体，并在需要时保存或删除媒体。你可以在 Android 设置中调整授权。

媒体扫描、照片指纹和清晰度评分、压缩、格式转换、裁剪、水印、移除 EXIF、生成 PDF 和文字识别（OCR）均在设备上处理。X App Team 不会收到你的媒体文件、照片元数据或识别出的文字。你保存文件、向其他应用分享导出内容或复制识别文字时，内容由所选存储位置、接收应用或 Android 剪贴板处理。你选用的文件提供方可能有自己的隐私政策。

Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit 在设备上识别文字。Google 说明，该 SDK 会通过 HTTPS 向 Google 发送诊断和使用指标，可能包括设备与应用信息、安装实例标识符、功能事件与错误码，以及处理时长、输入大小等性能指标。识别时，图片和识别出的文字保留在设备上。详见 ML Kit 服务条款与隐私说明和 ML Kit Android 数据披露。

SnapClean 无需注册 X App Team 账号，也没有开发者运营的媒体服务器或广告 SDK。X App Team 不出售你的个人信息。

本机存储、备份和删除

SnapClean 在 Android 应用存储中保存扫描索引、照片指纹、查看状态、偏好和清理统计。临时文件可能保存在应用缓存中。扫描数据库排除在 Android 云备份之外。设置等符合条件的其他应用数据可能根据 Android 的备份或设备迁移设置备份或恢复。

你可以在 SnapClean 设置中清除扫描索引，也可以在 Android 的应用存储设置中清除缓存或应用数据。卸载应用会移除应用的本机存储。保存到相册的文件、已分享的副本及系统备份需分别管理。Android 11 及以上版本会在媒体移入回收站前请求系统确认，保留和恢复方式由 Android 管理。较早版本上的删除可能是永久删除。
