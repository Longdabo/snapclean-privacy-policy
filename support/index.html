SnapClean Support

For help with SnapClean on iPhone or iPad, email longdabo@gmail.com. Include the app version from SnapClean Settings, your device and system version, and the steps that led to the issue. Keep private photos, videos, and recognized text out of your message.

Photos access

To change which photos SnapClean can scan, open your device’s Settings and find SnapClean under Apps. If an item is stored only in iCloud, open it in Photos first to download it to the device.

Review and recover photos

Items in SnapClean’s Review Bin are waiting for your decision. Restore them from the Review Bin before confirming deletion. After confirming deletion, manage recovery in Recently Deleted in Apple Photos.

Privacy policy

简体中文支持

使用 SnapClean 时遇到问题，请邮件联系 longdabo@gmail.com，说明 App 版本、设备和系统版本以及复现步骤。请勿附上私人照片、视频或识别文字。

你可在系统设置中更改照片授权。确认删除前，可在 SnapClean 的待清理列表中恢复项目。确认后，请到系统「照片」的「最近删除」查看。
